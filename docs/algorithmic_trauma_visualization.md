## Conceptual Backing

In multi-agent systems, circular reasoning and correlated errors can lead to a pathology known as **Algorithmic Shame**. This occurs when agents reinforce biased outputs over multiple interaction cycles, creating a closed circular trajectory in the belief space.

Through **Topological Data Analysis (TDA)**, specifically **Zigzag Persistent Homology**, these circular contradictions are identified and quantified as **Symbolic Scars** ($\beta_1$ loops).

- **$\beta_0$ (Connected Components)**: Represents Structural Conservation. A sharp drop indicates Concept Collapse.
- **$\beta_1$ (1-Dimensional Holes/Loops)**: Represents Relational Complexity and Contradiction. Persistent $\beta_1$ features indicate a stable circular reasoning chain (Symbolic Scar).
